0b. BLOCKER FOUND BY A REAL END-TO-END PURCHASE  (4 Sep 2026)
====================================================================

A real purchase was completed as a signed-in member and traced all the way to the
inbox. Fix these two before anything else in this document.

BLOCKER 1 - ALL OUTBOUND MEMBER EMAIL IS SILENTLY FAILING. REGRESSION.
  SCOPE IS WIDER THAN RECEIPTS. Two separate sends were triggered and traced to
  the inbox; both reported success in the UI and neither was delivered:
    (a) the purchase receipt and access email for order #9 - see below
    (b) the email-confirmation resend from /account, which printed
        "Sent. Give it a minute, and do check your spam folder."
        A search of in:anywhere newer_than:1h returned NOTHING.
  The pattern is the dangerous one: the send path returns success and renders a
  confident confirmation, so no one - not Yvette, not the customer - has any
  signal that mail is dead. Treat this as the top priority and fix the transport
  or the queue, then add real delivery reporting (provider message id, accepted /
  bounced / failed per message, and a visible failure state instead of "Sent").

  THIS ALSO BLOCKS A MEMBER FEATURE. Posting in the community and commenting on
  a lesson are both gated behind email confirmation:
      "Confirm your email address and you can post here. Everything below is
       yours to read in the meantime."
      "Please confirm your email address first. Check your inbox for the link."
  Because the confirmation email never arrives, a new member can never post or
  comment. The gate itself is well designed and degrades gracefully - the bug is
  purely that the email does not send.

BLOCKER 1a - THE PURCHASE EMAIL, SPECIFICALLY. REGRESSION.
  What was done: created coupon MADHAVFREE at 100% off; bought the live offer
  "ZZ Test - checkout probe" ($19, sells Practice Reset Intensive) as member
  sagarshankaranm@gmail.com. Order #9 completed at $0.00.
  What worked: the checkout adapted correctly to a free order - total $0.00, the
  payment section relabelled to "Finish up", the copy changed to "There's nothing
  to pay - finish below and your access is granted straight away", and the button
  became "GET INSTANT ACCESS" instead of "PAY". Entitlement was granted
  instantly. The coupon incremented to "Used 1 time". The success page said:
      "You're in. We charged $0.00 and sent a receipt to
       sagarshankaranm@gmail.com. Access details are on their way to your inbox."
  What failed: NO EMAIL WAS EVER SENT. Checked the inbox, spam and
  in:anywhere newer_than:1d - seven messages in the last day, none from Boss
  Clinician. No receipt, no access details, nothing.
  Why this is a bug and not a setting: Settings -> Payments & checkout has
  "Email customers a receipt" = ON and "Which payments get a receipt" =
  "Every payment, including renewals" (value "every"), NOT the
  "Only payments with a value above zero" option.
  Why this is a REGRESSION: order #7 on 18 Aug was also $0.00 via a 100% coupon
  and it delivered a correct, fully itemised receipt WITH a PDF attachment
  (receipt-2026-08-18-7.pdf) to the other account. So this pipeline worked before
  and has broken since - most likely in this week's receipt/billing changes.
  ACCEPTANCE: repeat the same 100%-off purchase and receive both the receipt
  (with PDF) and the access email. Then repeat with a real paid order. Then set
  the rule to "Only payments with a value above zero" and confirm a $0.00 order
  still gets its ACCESS email even when the receipt is intentionally suppressed -
  access details and receipts are different messages and must not share a switch.

BLOCKER 2 - THE PAYMENTS LIST DOES NOT SAY WHAT WAS BOUGHT.
  /admin/sales/payments shows both orders as "A one-off purchase" under the
  "What they bought" column, with only the buyer's email to distinguish them.
  FIX: show the offer name, and the products it granted. This column is the whole
  point of the screen.
